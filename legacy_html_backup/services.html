Clear Answers

Patient FAQs

Answers to your most common questions about dental treatments at Shri Dental Care.

Is Root Canal Treatment (RCT) painful?
No! With modern laser-assisted RCT and precise local anesthesia, the procedure is completely painless. Most patients feel instant relief from toothache after their first session.
How long do dental implants last?
Dental implants are designed to be a lifetime solution. Titanium fuses with living jawbone. With proper oral hygiene and regular checkups, implants can last 25+ years or more.
What is the difference between Braces and Clear Aligners?
Braces use fixed brackets and wires, while clear aligners are invisible, removable plastic trays. Both achieve excellent results — aligners offer greater comfort and aesthetic discretion.
Do you offer EMI or installment payment plans?
Yes! We offer flexible EMI options for high-value treatments like Dental Implants, Clear Aligners, and Full Smile Makeovers to make premium care accessible to every patient.
How many sessions does teeth whitening take?
Our professional LED-accelerated whitening delivers 6–10 shades brighter results in a single 45-minute session. Results last 12–24 months with good oral hygiene.
Can I visit the clinic on Sundays?
Absolutely! We are open all 7 days a week, Monday through Sunday from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Call 9892694771 / 9503703143 to book an appointment or walk in during clinic hours.
